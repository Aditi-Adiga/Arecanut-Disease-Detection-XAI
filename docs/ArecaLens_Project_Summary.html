1. Executive Summary & Purpose

ArecaLens is an end-to-end Explainable Artificial Intelligence (XAI) screening aid and knowledge-grounded agricultural assistant designed for arecanut (Areca catechu) growers in Karnataka and Kerala, India. The application targets critical fungal diseases—predominantly Mahali / Koleroga (fruit rot caused by Phytophthora meadii) and Yellow Leaf Disease (YLD).

Unlike generic crop apps that risk farmer livelihood through hallucinated pesticide doses or false reassurances, ArecaLens enforces strict protective principles: high-confidence thresholds (categorizing borderline scans as "Unsure"), zero chemical dosage invention, and complete offline determinism.

Engineering Core Tenet
"ArecaLens includes a knowledge-grounded retrieval assistant (not a generative one) that answers from cited, expert-review-pending sources with strict offline safety."

3. Computer Vision & Explainable AI (XAI) Pipeline

  • Target Classes & Protective Cutoffs:
    • Healthy_Leaf (Confidence ≥ 0.95)
    • Healthy_Nut (Confidence ≥ 0.95)
    • Mahali_Koleroga (Fruit rot, Confidence ≥ 0.70)
    • Yellow_Leaf (Yellow Leaf Disease, Confidence ≥ 0.70)
    • Scans scoring below thresholds are categorized as "Unsure" to prevent dangerous false reassurances.
  • Deterministic Class Activation Maps (CAM): Generates feature-weighted activation heatmaps (CAM = ReLU(features × Weff)) overlaid directly on raw photos to reveal visual lesion boundaries.
  • Preprocessing & Colab Parity: Bilinear resize to 224x224 with exact TensorFlow half-pixel centers (float32 [0, 255]), mathematically validated against Google Colab baseline exports.
  • Local Privacy: Retains up to 8 recent scans in browser localStorage; raw image uploads are never stored on server disks.

4. Multilingual Knowledge-Grounded Chatbot ("Ask" Tab)

  • 100% Deterministic Grounding: Standard-library engine (backend/chat_engine.py) answers strictly from verified sources (chatbot_faq.json, disease_info.json).
  • Result-Aware Context: Features an "About your last result" chip linking screening outcomes to follow-up care without re-transmitting photos.
  • Mandatory Safety Guardrails:
    • _DOSE: Refuses unverified spray quantities and concentrations; directs to agricultural officers and KVKs.
    • _HEALTH: Intercepts questions regarding human poisoning or ingestion, urging immediate emergency medical help.
    • _CONFIRM: Enforces that screening results are "suspected", never "confirmed".
    • _OUT: Politely declines unrelated queries (market prices, weather, other crops).
  • Multilingual Localization: Localized answers and dynamic suggestion chips across English (en), Kannada (kn), Hindi (hi), and Malayalam (ml) with clear draft-translation notices.

5. Online "Ask More" Cloud Proxy & RAG Safety Post-Checks

  • Server-Grounded Context: Automatically retrieves top-3 FAQ items and active class guidance as strict context for Google Gemini or Groq LLMs.
  • Strict Post-Generation Audit: Scans model output; immediately discards and reverts to verified guide text if the model outputs unverified units (ml, g, kg, litre, ppm, %) or certainty terms (confirmed, 100%).
  • Transparent Provenance: Clearly badges responses as "AI-generated • Verify with officer" vs "From the verified guide".

7. Companion Flutter Mobile Application

Located in the mobile/ directory, a Flutter cross-platform application complements the web platform. It features local farmer onboarding, 5-tab navigation, camera/gallery scanning, offline network indicators, and a pure Dart chat engine with 100% golden parity to the backend logic.
